## AZ-02

[2022 Chapter 120, House Bill 2026](https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/55leg/2R/laws/0120.htm), Section 1, amended 15-211(F), final sentence.

> The state board must give approval to a school district or charter school before any portion of the monies generated by the K-3 reading support level weight may be distributed to the school district or charter school pursuant to this subsection.

**Scope and conditions:** F applies to specified C, D, or F school grades or more than ten percent of third graders below the stated reading threshold. It requires department review and recommendation plus state-board approval; it is not a condition imposed here on every district's every dollar.
