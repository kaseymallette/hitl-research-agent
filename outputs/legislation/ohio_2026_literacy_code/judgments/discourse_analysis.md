All four occur in FOS; other dimensions have no substantive silencing observations. EL students themselves are present in 3313.608(A)(2)(a) and (I), pp. 6, 14. Those provisions qualify promotion and staffing; they do not articulate the pedagogical content claimed absent. The analysis correctly records that distinction and a separate counter-example.
4. **Structural causal account:** poverty, school-funding inequality, and racial segregation are absent as causes or remedial targets in 3313.608(B)(3)(a), p. 9, and elsewhere. Reduced teacher-student ratios are expressly present in (ii). State-funded summer services in (E), p. 12, also concern resources without explaining funding inequality as a cause. The analysis acknowledges the organizational lever and limits its claim to the unnamed causes.

The positive counter-examples—comprehension, writing, and EL recognition—are accurate and are not counted as failed absence claims. Likewise, reduced ratios are an acknowledged limit on the structural-silencing interpretation, not a hidden contradiction.

**Mismatch: PRE-1, “Universal benefit, without a guarantee of universal attainment.”** PRE-1 (Universal Learnability), marked partial, reasonably captures the limited universalizing pattern: benefit for all students from instruction directed toward effective reading. But the additional **PRE-4 (Research Consensus), partial**, does not fit. A categorical instructional-benefit statement does not itself presuppose that research is settled, unanimous, or conclusive. The analysis explicitly recognizes that the stronger claim is not printed; an uncertainty label does not supply that code's missing evidence. Retain PRE-1 (partial); for the additional consensus attribution, **no code fits**. This is a coding mismatch, not a false statement in the explanatory prose.

Other qualified uses fit within their stated limits. LEX-2 uses the explicit/systematic descriptors as a partial LEX-2 instance without claiming IDA adoption. PRE-2 treats the introductory requirement and exemptions as a differentiated knowledge need, not universal ignorance. FOS-5 uses FOS-6 only for unnamed structural causes, explicitly recognizing reduced ratios as a limit on its pattern. SUB-1 combines regulated participation with professional-development recognition; SUB-3 does not infer a new substantive legal right. These are qualified observations, not full-strength assertions of deficit, exclusive instructional causation, or unrestricted parental entitlement.

The three INT observations are appropriately marked **“no code fits.”** The codebook lacks codes for intrastate statutory incorporation, disability-law documentary authority, and administrative standards adopted by reference. They are not interstate borrowing, historical reading-policy precedents, or identified research citations. These three observations identify a codebook coverage gap; they are not code failures.

The analysis is faithful to the source, with all 20 observations grounded and no document-level factual errors found.
