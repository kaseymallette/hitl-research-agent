## A3 — Strength of language

**1/2 — localized inconsistency, with overwhelmingly preserved force.** This document-level rating includes claims outside the random sample; the sample itself produced no 1s.

The clear actor shift is **C31, step 10.1**: “The State Board and Office of Student Achievement shall establish” leadership-coach qualifications and duties. Section 20-2-270.3(g)(1), p. 7, assigns that duty to the **State Board, in collaboration with OSA**, rather than giving the two bodies a joint shall-duty. C5 and C27 preserve this distinction for the other coach types, so the treatment is inconsistent. A smaller compression occurs in C203, step 50.4: the additional-assessment comparator becomes “other students,” losing the source's explicit same-grade comparator (§20-2-151(b)(2)(D)(iv)(III), p. 33).

There is no systematic strengthening or weakening. The reading preserves conditional appropriations, open minimum qualification/duty lists, the two-to-five screener/materials ranges, the 70-percent classroom minimum, “as time permits,” discretionary multiple listings, strong encouragement for materials, optional readiness assessment, parental-election conditions, and the three distinct placement/retention pathways. It retains eligible-for-services versus receiving-services distinctions, appeal/unanimity requirements, grade ranges, and implementation dates. It also reports the bill's apparent deadline/cycle inconsistencies rather than silently harmonizing them.
