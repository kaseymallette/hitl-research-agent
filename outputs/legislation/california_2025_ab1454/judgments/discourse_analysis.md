## B5. Coverage

There are **2 LEX, 2 PRE, 4 FOS, 4 SUB, and 2 INT observations**, 14 overall. All five dimensions receive substantive treatment. PRE's relative thinness is explained by the absence of a separate findings section asserting crisis or research consensus. INT has only two observations, but these address the principal incorporated authorities and implementation texts; the analysis explains why the codebook's research, advocacy, and interstate patterns do not apply. Coverage extends through Section 4, with meaningful attention to the primary-language provision on the final page.

The following additional possibilities are **my own reading**, not required observations or factual failures:

- §60205(b)(4), p. 3, requires inclusion of materials addressing subsets of skills and standards, with clear designation of their coverage. In conjunction with §60205(d)(2), it could support a fuller LEX/FOS/SUB observation about literacy being divided into identifiable components and local agencies assembling combinations. PRE observation 1 mentions this requirement but does not develop its discourse implications.
- The materials deadline is January 31, 2027 (§60205(a)); the specialist preparation deadline is January 1, 2028 (§44265.1(a)); administrative preparation follows by September 1, 2028 (§44270.6(a)). This staged sequence could illuminate institutional priorities without implying an emergency narrative.
- The digest's distinction between existing law and new duties, its state-mandated-local-program characterization, and its constitutional reimbursement account could extend the PRE/INT account of institutional legitimacy. The analysis concentrates on enacted provisions rather than this prefatory framing.
