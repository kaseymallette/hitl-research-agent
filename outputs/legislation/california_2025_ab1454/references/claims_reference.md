Source: **Assembly Bill No. 1454, Chapter 471**, identified in the digest as **AB 1454, Rivas. Pupil literacy: credential program standards and professional development: instructional materials.** The heading describes an act adding Education Code Sections 44265.1, 44270.6, and 60205, relating to pupil literacy. Approved by the Governor October 09, 2025; filed with the Secretary of State October 09, 2025 (PDF p. 1).

## Operative provisions, in document order

### Section 1: new Education Code § 44265.1 (p. 2)

- **O01 — Amendment.** Section 1 adds § 44265.1 to the Education Code. Its contents are new text, not a revision of quoted preexisting language.
- **O02 — Requirement; authority; deadline; covered credentials (§ 44265.1(a)).** No later than **January 1, 2028**, the commission shall ensure that both its **standards of program quality and effectiveness** and its **teaching performance expectations** for preparing candidates for the **reading and literacy leadership specialist credential** and the **reading and literacy added authorization** include preparation on how to **deliver instruction and support teachers to deliver instruction** through effective means for teaching literacy. The preparation must align to **all** three numbered criteria in (a)(1)–(3), detailed below. Both credentials and both sets of preparation standards/expectations are within the duty.
- **O03 — Required criterion; named framework (§ 44265.1(a)(1)).** Alignment to the **English Language Arts/English Language Development (ELA/ELD) Framework**, including instruction to meet the varied language development needs of English learners.
- **O04 — Required criterion; instructional components; beneficiaries (§ 44265.1(a)(2)).** Alignment to evidence-based means of teaching foundational reading skills. These **shall include explicit and systematic instruction** in **print concepts, phonological awareness, phonics and word recognition, and fluency to all pupils**; and attending to **oral and written language development, vocabulary and background knowledge, and comprehension**, including **tiered supports for pupils with reading difficulties, English learners, and pupils with exceptional needs**. The enumerated content is mandatory; “include” does not make it an exclusive definition of evidence-based instruction.
- **O05 — Required criterion; statutory source (§ 44265.1(a)(3)).** Alignment to the **program guidelines for dyslexia developed pursuant to Section 56335**.
- **O06 — Legislative intent (§ 44265.1(b)).** The Legislature intends candidates for both the reading and literacy leadership specialist credential and the reading and literacy added authorization to be prepared to **deliver instruction and support teachers to deliver instruction** through effective means for teaching literacy **in programs in which English learner pupils are being instructed in their primary languages**. This is an intent statement, not a separately worded “shall” duty.

The outer (a)(1)–(3) list is a specified three-part list, **all** of which must be met. Its internal “including” language is inclusive, not an exhaustive limitation.

### Section 2: new Education Code § 44270.6 (pp. 2–3)

- **O07 — Amendment.** Section 2 adds § 44270.6 to the Education Code.
- **O08 — Requirement; authority; deadline; covered credential (§ 44270.6(a), p. 2).** No later than **September 1, 2028**, the commission shall ensure that the program standards for professional preparation of candidates for a **preliminary services credential with a specialization in administrative services**, established pursuant to **paragraph (3) of subdivision (a) of Section 44270**, include preparation on how to **support teachers in delivering instruction** through effective means for teaching literacy. The preparation must align to **all** three criteria in (a)(1)–(3), detailed below.
- **O09 — Required criterion; named framework (§ 44270.6(a)(1), p. 3).** Alignment to the **English Language Arts/English Language Development (ELA/ELD) Framework**, including instruction to meet the varied language development needs of English learners.
- **O10 — Required criterion; instructional components; beneficiaries (§ 44270.6(a)(2), p. 3).** Alignment to evidence-based means of teaching foundational reading skills, which **shall include explicit and systematic instruction** in **print concepts, phonological awareness, phonics and word recognition, and fluency to all pupils**; and attending to **oral and written language development, vocabulary and background knowledge, and comprehension**, including **tiered supports for pupils with reading difficulties, English learners, and pupils with exceptional needs**. These are required inclusions, not an exclusive definition.
- **O11 — Required criterion; statutory source (§ 44270.6(a)(3), p. 3).** Alignment to the **program guidelines for dyslexia developed pursuant to Section 56335**.
- **O12 — Requirement; implementation accountability (§ 44270.6(b), p. 3).** The commission **shall confirm that administrative preparation programs implement the standards pursuant to this section**. No separate date or particular confirmation procedure is specified here.
- **O13 — Legislative intent (§ 44270.6(c), p. 3).** The Legislature intends candidates for a preliminary services credential with a specialization in administrative services to be prepared to **support teachers to deliver instruction** through effective means for teaching literacy **in programs in which English learner pupils are being instructed in their primary languages**.

The outer (a)(1)–(3) list is a specified three-part list, **all** required. Internal “including” lists are inclusive. Unlike § 44265.1(a), this section’s preparation duty is worded as supporting teachers, and does not separately specify teaching performance expectations.

### Section 3: new Education Code § 60205 (pp. 3–4)

- **O14 — Amendment.** Section 3 adds § 60205 to the Education Code.
- **O15 — Requirement; authority; deadline; grades; subjects; procedure (§ 60205(a), p. 3).** **On or before January 31, 2027**, the state board **shall adopt instructional materials for kindergarten and grades 1 to 8, inclusive, in English language arts and English language development**, adhering to the procedures established pursuant to **Section 60227**.
- **O16 — Requirement; authority; complete set of adoption criteria (§ 60205(b), p. 3).** The state board **shall ensure** materials adopted pursuant to this section adhere to **all** four criteria in (b)(1)–(4), detailed below. These criteria are cumulative, not alternatives.
- **O17 — Required criterion; framework; language-development components (§ 60205(b)(1), p. 3).** Materials must align to the **current English Language Arts/English Language Development (ELA/ELD) Framework**, including **integrated and designated English language development instruction adopted by the state board**, and including **strategies to meet the varied language development needs of English learners**.
- **O18 — Required criterion; instructional components; beneficiaries (§ 60205(b)(2), p. 3).** Materials must align to evidence-based means of teaching foundational reading skills, which **shall include explicit and systematic instruction** in **print concepts, phonological awareness, phonics and word recognition, and fluency**; and attending to **oral and written language development, vocabulary and background knowledge, and comprehension**, including **tiered supports for pupils with reading difficulties, English learners, and pupils with exceptional needs**. These are mandatory inclusions within an inclusive formulation. Unlike the parallel credential provisions, this paragraph does not print “to all pupils” after “fluency.”
- **O19 — Required criterion; statutory source (§ 60205(b)(3), p. 3).** Materials must align to the **program guidelines for dyslexia developed pursuant to Section 56335**.
- **O20 — Required criterion; subset materials and designation (§ 60205(b)(4), p. 3).** Include materials focused on teaching **specific skills and standards that are a subset of the entire English Language Arts/English Language Development (ELA/ELD) Framework**, and **clearly designate which standards they effectively address**. Both inclusion of such materials and designation of the standards addressed are specified.
- **O21 — Requirement; authority; triggering event; guidance (§ 60205(c), p. 3).** **Upon adoption** of instructional materials pursuant to subdivision (a), the state board **shall update the state Guidance for Local Instructional Materials Adoptions** to reflect the criteria in subdivision (b).
- **O22 — Requirement; local adoption process (§ 60205(c), p. 3).** A **local educational agency shall follow this guidance and the processes described in Section 60002** for purposes of adopting instructional materials. Both the updated guidance and the statutory processes apply.
- **O23 — Conditional requirement; certification/accountability (§ 60205(d)(1), p. 3).** A local educational agency **that adopts instructional materials that have not been adopted by the state board pursuant to subdivision (a)** **shall certify** that those materials align to the criteria in subdivision (b). The provision attaches certification to this local adoption condition; it does not prohibit adopting such materials. It specifies no recipient, form, or separate certification deadline.
- **O24 — Permission; condition on combined materials (§ 60205(d)(2), p. 3).** A local educational agency **may adopt a set of instructional materials that, in combination, address all of the state standards and components of the English Language Arts/English Language Development (ELA/ELD) Framework**. The permission allows combined coverage; “all” retains the complete-coverage condition.
- **O25 — Legislative intent; primary-language materials and purpose (§ 60205(e), p. 4).** The Legislature intends the state board’s adoption of English language arts and English language development instructional materials to **include high-quality instructional materials in pupils’ primary languages**, **to enable teachers to build upon pupils’ linguistic assets**, in programs **for which English learner pupils are being instructed in their primary language**. This is legislative intent, not a separate mandatory adoption command.

The outer (b)(1)–(4) list has four specified, required criteria. Internal “including” clauses require the named content without stating that nothing else may be included. Subdivision (d)(2) requires “all” state standards and framework components to be addressed by the permitted combination; subdivision (b)(4) expressly accommodates materials addressing a subset.

### Section 4: state-mandated costs (p. 4)

- **O26 — Conditional requirement; funding; authority; consequence.** **If the Commission on State Mandates determines that this act contains costs mandated by the state**, reimbursement **to local agencies and school districts for those costs shall be made pursuant to Part 7 (commencing with Section 17500) of Division 4 of Title 2 of the Government Code**. The Commission determines whether the condition exists; reimbursement is conditional, not an unconditional grant. No dollar amount, appropriation, reimbursement date, or newly specified payment procedure is printed.

## Legislative Counsel’s Digest and front matter (pp. 1–2)

These entries inventory what the document says outside the enactment. Descriptions of existing law are reported as descriptions, without importing the cited laws’ unprinted details.

- **D01 — Existing credentialing authority (p. 1, digest opening paragraph).** Existing law requires the **Commission on Teacher Credentialing** to establish standards for issuing and renewing **credentials, certificates, and permits**.
- **D02 — Existing administrative credential requirements (p. 1, same paragraph).** Existing law sets minimum requirements for a preliminary services credential specializing in administrative services. These include, **among other requirements**, completion of **an entry-level program of specialized and professional preparation in administrative services OR a one-year internship in a program of supervised training in administrative services**, subject to commission approval, as provided. This is an open minimum-requirements list with two stated alternatives, not a new requirement imposed by this act.
- **D03 — Existing literacy credential requirements (p. 1, same paragraph).** Existing law sets minimum requirements for the reading and literacy leadership specialist credential and reading and literacy added authorization, including, **among other requirements**, completion of a program of specialized and professional preparation, as provided. The list is open.
- **D04 — Summary of administrative preparation change (p. 1, second digest paragraph).** The bill would require the commission, no later than **September 1, 2028**, to ensure the administrative credential preparation program standards meet specified criteria, **including, among other things**, preparation on supporting teachers in delivering instruction through effective means for teaching literacy. This summarizes O08–O11 rather than adding another duty.
- **D05 — Summary of literacy preparation change (p. 1, second digest paragraph).** The bill would require the commission, no later than **January 1, 2028**, to ensure literacy leadership specialist/added-authorization program standards and teaching performance expectations include preparation to deliver instruction and support teachers to deliver instruction through effective means for teaching literacy. This summarizes O02–O05.
- **D06 — Existing state instructional-materials duty (p. 1, third digest paragraph).** Existing law requires the state board to adopt basic instructional materials for **kindergarten and grades 1–8, inclusive**, for **governing boards of school districts and county boards of education**, subject to specified provisions. These include, **among others**, the **State Board of Education** adopting **at least 5 basic instructional materials for all applicable grade levels in each specified subject area**. Subject areas include, **among others**, language arts, **including, but not limited to, spelling, reading, and English language development**. Both the subject-area examples and the language-arts examples are open lists. The five-material minimum is described as existing law, not newly written into § 60205.
- **D07 — Summary of materials adoption and criteria (p. 1, fourth digest paragraph).** The bill would require adoption by **January 31, 2027**, adhering to specified provisions concerning **followup adoptions**, of materials for **kindergarten and grades 1–8, inclusive**, in English language arts and English language development; and would require the state board to ensure adopted materials meet specified criteria. This summarizes O15–O20.
- **D08 — Summary of guidance and local duties; local-program consequence (p. 1, fourth digest paragraph).** Upon adoption, the bill would require updating the state **Guidance for Local Instructional Materials Adoptions** to reflect the criteria; require local educational agencies to follow the guidance for adoption; and require agencies adopting materials not adopted by the state board to certify alignment to the criteria. The digest says these additional local educational agency duties **would impose a state-mandated local program**. See O21–O23 for operative wording and conditions.
- **D09 — Existing reimbursement basis (p. 2, opening paragraph).** The **California Constitution** requires the state to reimburse local agencies and school districts for **certain costs mandated by the state**; statutory provisions establish reimbursement procedures. No constitutional article or section is given.
- **D10 — Reimbursement summary (p. 2, second paragraph).** If the **Commission on State Mandates** determines the bill contains state-mandated costs, reimbursement shall be made under the statutory provisions described above. See O26 for the enacted citation.
- **D11 — Digest Key (p. 2).** **Vote: majority; Appropriation: no; Fiscal Committee: yes; Local Program: yes.** These are printed legislative classifications, not dollar allocations or additional operative duties.
- **D12 — Enacting authority (p. 2).** The enacting clause identifies **the People of the State of California** as enacting the following text.

## Category and amendment checks

- **Definitions:** No express defined-term provision appears. Credential names, framework names, and the ELA/ELD abbreviation are used; the act does not separately define them. The required instructional inclusions are inventoried as criteria rather than as an exhaustive definition.
- **Findings and purpose:** The act contains three express legislative-intent statements, O06, O13 and O25. It contains no separate findings section or empirical findings. The heading identifies pupil literacy as the subject.
- **Requirements and assigned authority:** O02–O05, O08–O12, O15–O23 and O26 retain the actors, mandatory components, triggering conditions and deadlines. Component entries within these groups elaborate their parent duties.
- **Permissions:** O24 is the operative “may” provision. No “should” provision or separately worded encouragement appears.
- **Prohibitions:** No express prohibition appears. In particular, local adoption of non-state-adopted materials is subject to certification, not forbidden.
- **Consequences/accountability:** Confirmation of program implementation (O12), updating/following adoption guidance and processes (O21–O22), conditional certification (O23), and conditional reimbursement (O26). The digest also characterizes the bill as imposing a state-mandated local program (D08). No penalty, retention, notification duty, reporting schedule, or intervention regime is specified.
- **Exceptions/waivers:** No express exemption or waiver appears. O23’s certification condition, O24’s combined-materials permission, and O26’s reimbursement condition should retain their stated scope; they are not general waivers.
- **Funding and timing:** Adoption by January 31, 2027 (O15); literacy preparation standards/expectations by January 1, 2028 (O02); administrative preparation standards by September 1, 2028 (O08); guidance update upon adoption (O21); conditional reimbursement (O26). Approval and filing dates are October 09, 2025. The PDF states **no appropriation** in the Digest Key, supplies no dollar amount, and prints **no express effective date**. An effective date must not be inferred from outside law.
- **Amendments and deletions:** The act adds §§ 44265.1, 44270.6 and 60205 in full. It prints no struck-through provision, deletion, repeal, or word-level replacement. Section 4 is a separate reimbursement provision. Existing-law descriptions in the digest are neither struck text nor enactments of those existing requirements.
