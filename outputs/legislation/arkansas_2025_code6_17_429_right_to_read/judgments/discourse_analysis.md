Along with performance bonuses for coaches in (j)(1)(D)(xii), this extends assessment-based accountability beyond individual student plans and district sanctions.

- **INT:** References to accreditation, assessment, professional-development, and funding statutes in (b)(2), (h), (i), and (k)(1)(A) show further reliance on existing administrative law. The three selected intertexts are sound, but do not exhaust this legal network.
