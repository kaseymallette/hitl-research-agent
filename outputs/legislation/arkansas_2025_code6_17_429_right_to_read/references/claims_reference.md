# Provisions reference: article.pdf

## Scope and reading conventions

Source: the supplied 18-page document, headed **2025 Arkansas Code, Title 6 — Education, Subtitle 2 — Elementary and Secondary Education Generally, Chapter 17 — Personnel, Subchapter 4 — Licensure Generally, § 6-17-429. Right to Read Act — Definitions**. Its printed universal citation is **AR Code § 6-17-429 (2025)**. This inventory was prepared from article.txt and all page images in pages/, independently of any close reading or judgment and without consulting external sources.

The operative provisions begin with (a) on PDF page 1 and end with (p) on page 17. The History on page 17 is inventoried separately as source metadata; the publisher's disclaimer is recorded separately as nonoperative material. Page 18 is blank. There are no fiscal notes, summaries, appendices, or marked amendment passages. Navigation controls and publisher branding are not legislative provisions.

Entries below follow document order and give subsection and PDF page locations. They paraphrase unless expressly quoted. Labels identify the rubric's overlapping coverage types: **definition; finding/purpose; requirement; prohibition; permission; authority; criteria/list; consequence; exception; funding/timing; named source; amendment**. An entry can belong to more than one type; its individually identified subcomponents remain part of the inventory. An enumerated list is described as closed where the printed enumeration gives the specified alternatives/components without an expansion formula; this does not imply a prohibition on additional actions unless the text supplies one. “Including without limitation” and “not limited to” are expressly open. Lists with a residual category are identified as such.

For concision only, **division** means the named Division of Elementary and Secondary Education, **state board** means the State Board of Education, and **district/charter** means public school district and/or open-enrollment public charter school as the particular provision specifies. These are reference-list shorthand, not additional statutory definitions. “Family recipients” below denotes parents, legal guardians, or persons standing in loco parentis to a student where all three appear in the source; provisions referring only to parents retain that narrower wording. Do not conflate separate provisions that use different actors or modal force.

The source has wording that this inventory deliberately does not repair: overlapping teacher categories in (c)(1)(A)–(B); “or” in (l)(1); “independent” in (k)(3) versus “individual” in (k)(2); and the passive clauses in (l)(3)(E)–(F). These are noted at their locations.

## Ordered provision inventory

### (a): Name (page 1)

- **A01 — (a), p. 1 [requirement; permission].** The section shall be known and may be cited as the “Right to Read Act”.

### (b): Preparation and licensure (pages 1–2)

- **B01 — (b)(1)(A)(i)–(ii), pp. 1–2 [requirement; criteria/list].** Both listed categories shall have proficient knowledge and skills to teach reading consistent with the best practices of scientific reading instruction: (i) a person completing a state-approved educator preparation program; and (ii) a person seeking teacher licensure by reciprocity or by adding an endorsement. These are the two enumerated categories; reciprocity and adding an endorsement are alternatives within (ii).
- **B02 — (b)(1)(B), p. 2 [requirement; criteria/list].** A person completing a state-approved educator preparation program other than a teacher of elementary education program shall demonstrate awareness of the best practices of scientific reading instruction. Record this alongside B01 rather than silently rewriting B01's scope.
- **B03 — (b)(2), p. 2 [requirement; criteria/list; named source].** Each state-approved educator preparation program shall post on its website information describing its program to prepare teachers to teach reading using scientific reading instruction. That instruction must be aligned with, **but is not limited to**, content measured by the stand-alone reading assessment adopted by the State Board of Education under **§ 6-17-402**. The assessment content is an open alignment reference, not the entirety of required program content.

### (c): Required professional development and nonprovision (page 2)

- **C01 — (c)(1)(A), p. 2 [requirement; criteria/list].** Each public school district and open-enrollment public charter school shall provide scientific-reading-instruction professional development for one of the prescribed pathways to a **proficiency credential** in knowledge and practices in scientific reading instruction for the three enumerated groups: elementary-level K–6 licensed teachers; K–12 special-education licensed teachers; and K–12 licensed reading specialists.
- **C02 — (c)(1)(B), p. 2 [requirement; criteria/list].** Those same entities shall provide professional development for one of the prescribed pathways to an **awareness credential** in knowledge and practices in scientific reading instruction for the groups printed here: teachers licensed at a level other than the elementary level in K–6; teachers with a K–12 special-education license; and K–12 licensed reading specialists. The latter two categories are repeated from (A) in the document; do not replace this list with an inferred “all other teachers” category.
- **C03 — (c)(2)(A), p. 2 [requirement; consequence].** Each **public school** or open-enrollment public charter school failing to provide the professional development under (c)(1) **shall** be placed in probationary status. The actor is printed as public school here, and this mandatory consequence is distinct from the discretionary probation in (i)(1)(A).
- **C04 — (c)(2)(B), p. 2 [requirement; consequence].** Each such nonproviding public school or open-enrollment public charter school shall give parents notice that the public school district has not met this section's requirements. The source does not specify a written format in this clause.

### (d): Teacher credentials, timing, support, exemption (page 3)

- **D01 — (d)(1)(A)(i), p. 3 [requirement; criteria/list; funding/timing].** By the beginning of the **2023–2024 school year**, all teachers employed in classroom teaching positions requiring either a license to teach elementary students in **K–6**, or a license to teach a special-education course **directly related to literacy** for students in **K–12**, shall demonstrate proficiency in knowledge and practices of scientific reading instruction. The teaching-position and literacy-course qualifications are part of the rule.
- **D02 — (d)(1)(A)(ii), p. 3 [permission; authority; consequence; funding/timing; named source].** A teacher described in D01 who has not demonstrated proficiency by the 2023–2024 school year **may** be afforded an opportunity to do so through intensive support status under **§ 6-17-2807**. The teacher's evaluator specifies the period in the teacher's professional growth plan. Neither the opportunity nor a fixed support duration is made universal here.
- **D03 — (d)(1)(B), p. 3 [requirement; funding/timing].** By the beginning of the **2023–2024 school year**, all other teachers shall demonstrate awareness in knowledge and practices of scientific reading instruction.
- **D04 — (d)(2)(A)–(B)(ii), p. 3 [requirement; criteria/list; funding/timing].** All teachers shall demonstrate the proficiency or awareness applicable to their teaching position by completing the prescribed credential, either (A) as a condition of licensure, or (B) within **one year** if already licensed or employed under a waiver from licensure. These are the two stated timing routes; (B) contains two alternative qualifying statuses. No starting event for the one-year period beyond this wording is supplied.
- **D05 — (d)(3)(A)–(B), p. 3 [exception; prohibition; criteria/list].** This subsection's requirements **shall not apply** to a teacher who both (A) has already demonstrated proficiency through an approved, prescribed pathway **and** (B) is adding an endorsement to the teaching license. Both conditions are necessary; this is not an exemption from the whole section.

### (e): Preparation-provider reporting (page 4)

- **E01 — (e), p. 4 [requirement; funding/timing; named source].** A provider of a state-approved educator preparation program, graduate program, or alternative preparation program shall include in its **annual report to the Division of Elementary and Secondary Education** a description of its program to prepare educators to teach reading using scientific reading instruction. All three program categories are specified.

### (f): Approved materials, resources, curriculum (page 4)

- **F01 — (f), p. 4 [requirement; authority; criteria/list].** The division shall identify and create an approved list of materials, resources, and curriculum programs for public school districts and open-enrollment public charter schools. Listed items must be supported by the science of reading and based on instruction that is **explicit, systematic, cumulative, and diagnostic** (all four characteristics). The program categories in F02–F04 are **open**, introduced by “including without limitation”.
- **F02 — (f)(1)(A)–(B), p. 4 [criteria/list; named source].** Included dyslexia programs must be evidence-based and either aligned to **structured literacy** or grounded in the **Orton-Gillingham methodology**. These are alternative qualifications within this category, not two cumulative requirements.
- **F03 — (f)(2), p. 4 [criteria/list].** The approved-list categories include evidence-based reading intervention programs.
- **F04 — (f)(3), p. 4 [criteria/list].** The approved-list categories include evidence-based reading programs grounded in the science of reading.

### (g): Curriculum purchase, alternative approval, notice (page 4)

- **G01 — (g)(1), p. 4 [requirement; criteria/list].** A public school district and open-enrollment public charter school purchasing a curriculum program shall choose one from the division's approved list created under (f), read with the alternative-approval procedure in G02.
- **G02 — (g)(2)(A)–(B), p. 4 [requirement; exception; authority; criteria/list].** A district or charter choosing to purchase an off-list curriculum program shall submit to the division, **for approval** of the alternative program, both (A) a rationale for the choice and (B) evidence-based research regarding the alternative. This is a two-component submission; it does not say that submission alone is approval or that notice alone permits the purchase.
- **G03 — (g)(3), p. 4 [requirement; consequence].** A district or charter **using** a curriculum program outside the division's (f) list shall notify **all parents, legal guardians, and persons standing in loco parentis to students**, both **in writing and on its website**. This use-triggered notice is separate from the purchase-triggered approval submission.

### (h): Continuing professional development (page 5)

- **H01 — (h), (h)(1)–(2), p. 5 [requirement; criteria/list; funding/timing; named source].** Each public school district and open-enrollment public charter school shall establish a professional development program as required by **§ 6-15-2914(b)(1)(B)**. It shall (1) include, **without limitation**, instruction based on the science of reading, and (2) be provided **annually after completion of** the professional development required by (c)(1). These are two mandatory features, with expressly open instructional content.

### (i): Accreditation, provider accountability, prohibited instruction (pages 5–6)

- **I01 — (i)(1)(A), p. 5 [requirement; permission; authority; consequence; named source].** A district or charter employing an educator in violation of this section **or** failing to provide the required professional development **shall be in violation** of the **Standards for Accreditation of Arkansas Public Schools and School Districts** and **may** be placed in probationary status **by the division**. The accreditation violation is mandatory in the stated circumstances; probation under this clause is discretionary.
- **I02 — (i)(1)(B), p. 5 [requirement; consequence].** A district or charter placed on probation under (i)(1)(A) shall send **written notification** to parents of students in the public school district explaining the reason for probation.
- **I03 — (i)(2)(A), p. 5 [permission; consequence; criteria/list].** A noncomplying provider of a state-approved educator preparation program, graduate program, or alternative preparation program **may** face penalties **up to and including revocation of approval status**. Revocation is the stated maximum sanction, not an automatic consequence; lesser penalties are not exhaustively enumerated.
- **I04 — (i)(2)(B), p. 5 [requirement; authority; consequence; funding/timing].** The division shall audit **each program under (i)(2)(A)** at least **once every three years** to verify compliance. The reference is to that printed class of programs; the audit is not described as annual.
- **I05 — (i)(3)(A)(i)–(iii), pp. 5–6 [prohibition; criteria/list; definition].** A district or charter shall not use a program of instruction for **K–2 students** based in any practice or intervention program utilizing any of these three enumerated alternatives: (i) the three-cueing system model of reading; (ii) visual memory **as the primary basis for teaching word recognition**; or (iii) the three-cueing system model based on **meaning, structure and syntax, and visual**, also known as **“MSV”**. This is the specified three-item prohibition list. The MSV equivalence describes the named model; the clause does not forbid every use of visual memory or extend its grade range beyond K–2.
- **I06 — (i)(3)(B)(i), p. 6 [requirement; authority; consequence].** If the state board determines a district or charter has violated (i)(3)(A), the state board shall notify that entity of its violation.
- **I07 — (i)(3)(B)(ii)(a), p. 6 [requirement; authority; consequence; funding/timing; named source].** If the entity fails to remedy its (i)(3)(A) violation **within 60 days of notification** of failure to comply, the state board shall direct the division to withhold **a maximum of 10% of the monthly distribution of state foundation funding aid**, as provided under **§ 6-20-2305(a)(1)(B)**. Ten percent is a ceiling, not an invariably exact reduction, and the base is the monthly foundation-aid distribution.
- **I08 — (i)(3)(B)(ii)(b), p. 6 [requirement; authority; consequence; funding/timing].** Once the state board determines the district or charter has complied with this section, the division shall restore the monthly distribution of state foundation funding aid to its original amount before the (i)(3)(B)(ii)(a) reduction. The text requires restoration of the distribution amount; it does not expressly require repayment of prior withheld sums.

### (j)(1): Literacy coaches (pages 6–8)

- **J01 — (j) introductory clause, p. 6 [requirement; funding/timing].** The division's duties in **both (j)(1) and (j)(2)** begin with the **2023–2024 school year**, with full implementation **no later than the 2025–2026 school year**.
- **J02 — (j)(1)(A), pp. 6–7 [requirement; authority; criteria/list; finding/purpose; named source].** The division shall ensure access to a literacy coach for **every K–3 teacher** in either a public school earning a **“D” or “F”** rating under **§ 6-15-2105**, **§ 6-15-2106**, or state board rules, or a low-performing public school identified by the division based on its K–3 literacy-screener results under this section. The stated purpose is increased literacy rates through coaching for **teachers and administrators**. The rating and screener routes are alternatives; access is not stated for every teacher statewide.
- **J03 — (j)(1)(B), p. 7 [requirement; authority; criteria/list; exception; funding/timing].** **Subject to legislative appropriation**, the division shall provide, train, and assign literacy coaches to low-performing public schools based on their K–3 literacy-screener results under this section; the clause specifies criteria established by the division. No appropriation amount is enacted here.
- **J04 — (j)(1)(C)(i)–(iv), p. 7 [requirement; criteria/list; named source].** A literacy coach shall have, **without limitation**, all four listed qualifications: (i) expertise in pedagogy **and** science of reading; (ii) evidence of success in coaching **and** classroom instruction; (iii) understanding of reading learning disabilities; and (iv) understanding of the child find mandate of the **Individuals with Disabilities Education Act, 20 U. S. C. § 1400 et seq., as it existed on January 1, 2023**. This qualification list is expressly open; the federal-law date is part of the reference.
- **J05 — (j)(1)(D)(i), p. 7 [requirement; finding/purpose].** A literacy coach shall use knowledge of evidence-based instruction and practices aligned to science of reading to support teachers in maximizing student learning.
- **J06 — (j)(1)(D)(ii), p. 7 [requirement; finding/purpose].** A coach shall engage in instructional coaching cycles with teachers to build capacity for classroom instructional practices.
- **J07 — (j)(1)(D)(iii), p. 7 [requirement].** A coach shall deliver consistent and frequent job-embedded professional learning.
- **J08 — (j)(1)(D)(iv), p. 7 [requirement; finding/purpose].** A coach shall design and facilitate relevant and cohesive professional learning sessions to strengthen teachers' implementation of evidence-based instructional practices aligned to science of reading.
- **J09 — (j)(1)(D)(v), p. 7 [requirement].** A coach shall assist teachers with data analysis to inform instructional adjustments.
- **J10 — (j)(1)(D)(vi), p. 7 [requirement].** A coach shall partner with teachers to integrate professional learning into classroom practice.
- **J11 — (j)(1)(D)(vii), p. 8 [requirement].** A coach shall work with teachers to ensure effective communication strategies and resource sharing with parents, legal guardians, and persons standing in loco parentis to students.
- **J12 — (j)(1)(D)(viii), p. 8 [requirement].** A coach shall partner with a public school principal or designated leader to connect school-wide literacy goals with evidence-based instruction and practices aligned to science of reading.
- **J13 — (j)(1)(D)(ix), p. 8 [requirement; permission; consequence].** A coach shall provide feedback on teachers' evidence-based instruction and practices. That feedback **may** be used for teacher evaluations; evaluative use is permitted, not required.
- **J14 — (j)(1)(D)(x), p. 8 [requirement; finding/purpose].** A coach shall actively participate in professional learning experiences to deepen coaching knowledge and skills.
- **J15 — (j)(1)(D)(xi), p. 8 [requirement; authority; funding/timing; criteria/list].** A coach shall be compensated on a **competitive salary schedule determined by the division**. No base-salary amount is stated.
- **J16 — (j)(1)(D)(xii), p. 8 [requirement; criteria/list; funding/timing].** A coach shall receive a **yearly bonus of up to $10,000**, based on **measurable performance outcomes**. The amount is capped and performance-linked, not an unconditional $10,000 award.
- **J17 — (j)(1)(D)(xiii), p. 8 [requirement; authority; criteria/list].** A coach shall meet performance criteria established by the division. J05–J17 are the thirteen enumerated (D) duties/conditions; the enumeration contains no “without limitation” formula.
- **J18 — (j)(1)(E), p. 8 [permission; authority; criteria/list].** A coach **may** be employed by the division **directly or by contract**, the two specified alternatives.
- **J19 — (j)(1)(F), p. 8 [requirement; authority].** Public school districts and open-enrollment public charter schools identified by the division for coach access under (j)(1)(A) shall agree to involve the public school, district, and charter leadership team **as directed by the division**.

### (j)(2): Literacy tutoring grants (pages 8–11)

- **T01 — (j)(2)(A)(i), p. 8 [requirement; authority; funding/timing; finding/purpose].** The division shall establish a literacy tutoring grant program **to fund supplemental educational services for eligible students**, subject to the (j) implementation timetable in J01.
- **T02 — (j)(2)(A)(ii)(a)–(c), pp. 8–9 [definition; authority; criteria/list; exception; funding/timing].** “Eligible student” means **any student enrolled in public school in K–3** who meets one of three enumerated alternatives: (a) is determined not to meet the reading standard **as defined by the state board**; (b) is determined at risk for reading difficulties according to a **high-quality literacy screener** under this section; **or** (c) has received a good cause exemption for promotion to grade 4, **pending availability of funds**. The funds qualifier is printed at the end of (c); retain its location rather than erase it or silently recast the definition's enrollment/grade limits. This is a closed enumeration of three routes to eligibility.
- **T03 — (j)(2)(B), p. 9 [requirement; criteria/list; funding/timing].** The grant shall be **$1,500 per eligible student per year**, on a **first-come, first-served** basis, **with priority to eligible students who are to be retained in grade 3**. Preserve both the ordering rule and the priority.
- **T04 — (j)(2)(C) introductory clause, p. 9 [requirement; authority; criteria/list].** The division shall administer and implement the grant according to rules **developed and adopted by the state board**. Those rules **shall require the division** to perform the enumerated (i)–(viii) functions below. The enumeration gives eight functions, several with further mandatory subclauses; no general “without limitation” introduces the eight-item list.
- **T05 — (j)(2)(C)(i), p. 9 [requirement; authority].** The division must evaluate a student's grant eligibility.
- **T06 — (j)(2)(C)(ii), p. 9 [requirement; authority].** The division must develop an application process for **students and providers** and accept applications.
- **T07 — (j)(2)(C)(iii)(a), p. 9 [requirement; authority; criteria/list; consequence].** The division must evaluate supplemental-educational-service providers for their **initial and continued eligibility for payments**, including **without limitation** providers with early-literacy expertise. The provider examples are open.
- **T08 — (j)(2)(C)(iii)(b), p. 9 [requirement; authority; criteria/list; consequence; finding/purpose; named source].** The division shall establish minimum evaluation criteria for approved providers to ensure effectiveness in improving eligible students' reading abilities. Approved providers **shall be evaluated** by those criteria. Criteria include, **without limitation**, performance on the **Arkansas annual reading assessment** or **other division-approved literacy assessments**; this is an open assessment-performance list.
- **T09 — (j)(2)(C)(iii)(c), p. 9 [requirement; prohibition; consequence; funding/timing].** A provider failing to demonstrate improvement in eligible students' reading abilities for **two consecutive years shall be deemed ineligible** to participate in the program.
- **T10 — (j)(2)(C)(iv)(a), p. 10 [requirement; authority; funding/timing].** The division must remit payments to **approved providers for services rendered to eligible students** in the grant program.
- **T11 — (j)(2)(C)(iv)(b), p. 10 [requirement; authority; criteria/list; funding/timing].** The division shall establish criteria for prioritizing eligible students **if applicants exceed available grant funding**. The section does not itself print the additional prioritization criteria.
- **T12 — (j)(2)(C)(v), p. 10 [requirement; criteria/list; funding/timing].** The division must notify the governing authority of **each district and charter** of the grant application **process, requirements, and deadlines** for parents, legal guardians, or persons standing in loco parentis. These are the three specified notification topics; this clause supplies no calendar deadline for applications.
- **T13 — (j)(2)(C)(vi) introductory clause, p. 10 [requirement; permission; authority; criteria/list; funding/timing; finding/purpose].** The division must remit payment for services provided **up to $1,500 per eligible student per school year**. Funds **may** be used for the following specified purposes designed to improve reading or literacy skills: tutoring in T14 **or** digital programs in T15. This is a closed enumeration of two use categories, not an unrestricted educational allowance. Preserve the distinction between this payment cap and the grant amount wording in T03.
- **T14 — (j)(2)(C)(vi)(a)(1)–(2), p. 10 [permission; criteria/list].** Permitted use: **online or in-person, high-dosage tutoring**, from the state-approved provider list. Provider employees must be **trained in science of reading** and hold either (1) valid teaching certificates in **elementary education or reading**, or (2) baccalaureate or graduate degrees in **education, English, or another subject indicative of reading/literacy expertise**. Training is cumulative with either credential route. The two credential routes are enumerated; degree subjects include an express residual subject category.
- **T15 — (j)(2)(C)(vi)(b), p. 10 [permission; criteria/list].** Alternative permitted use: **evidence-based digital literacy applications or software programs**, from the **state-approved program list**, aligned with science of reading.
- **T16 — (j)(2)(C)(vii), p. 10 [requirement; authority; criteria/list; funding/timing].** The division must develop and curate a list of **approved tutoring providers and evidence-based digital literacy applications or software programs aligned with science of reading**. The list will be updated **regularly**; no numerical update interval is specified.
- **T17 — (j)(2)(C)(viii), p. 11 [requirement; authority; consequence].** The division must develop a procedure to verify that eligible grant recipients actually received the services or materials paid for.
- **T18 — (j)(2)(D)(i)–(iii), p. 11 [requirement; criteria/list; exception; funding/timing].** Each district and charter shall notify a student's parent, legal guardian, or person standing in loco parentis **no later than October 1 each year**, or **as soon as practicable if the reading need is identified after October 1**, regarding (i) each student's program eligibility, (ii) the application process, and (iii) other information supplied by the division. These are the three notification categories; the third leaves the content open to division-supplied information. This clause does not expressly prescribe written notice.

### (k): Progress notices, reading plans, intervention reporting (pages 11–13)

- **K01 — (k) introductory clause, p. 11 [requirement; funding/timing].** The duties on public school districts and open-enrollment public charter schools under (k) begin with the **2023–2024 school year**, with full implementation **no later than the 2025–2026 school year**. The specific (k)(4) reporting deadline is separately recorded in K13.
- **K02 — (k)(1)(A), p. 11 [requirement; consequence; funding/timing; named source].** Districts and charters shall notify **all parents, legal guardians, or persons standing in loco parentis**, **in writing and in a parent-friendly manner**, of their student's reading progress **each time the student is assessed throughout the year**, using high-quality literacy-screener results, as required by **§ 6-15-2907(a)(2)** and **§ 6-15-2006(a)(1)(C)**.
- **K03 — (k)(1)(B), p. 11 [permission].** This parent-friendly written notification **may** be in the language of communication preferred by a parent, legal guardian, or person standing in loco parentis. Preferred-language use is permissive in the document.
- **K04 — (k)(2)(A)(i)–(ii), pp. 11–12 [requirement; authority; criteria/list].** Districts and charters shall develop an **individual reading plan** for **each K–3 student not meeting the reading standard**, as (i) determined by the state board **and** (ii) measured by a **high-quality literacy screener or the state annual accountability assessment**. The two standard conditions are conjunctive, with alternative measurement instruments within (ii).
- **K05 — (k)(2)(B)(i)(a)–(e), p. 12 [requirement; criteria/list].** The plan shall include the student's **specific, diagnosed reading-skill needs**, including **without limitation**: (a) phonemic awareness; (b) phonics decoding; (c) text-reading fluency; (d) vocabulary-building strategies; and (e) self-regulated use of reading-comprehension strategies, as identified by high-quality literacy-screener data. The five-item skill-needs list is expressly open.
- **K06 — (k)(2)(B)(ii), p. 12 [requirement; criteria/list].** The plan shall include goals and benchmarks for the student's growth.
- **K07 — (k)(2)(B)(iii), p. 12 [requirement; criteria/list; consequence].** The plan shall include how progress will be monitored and evaluated.
- **K08 — (k)(2)(B)(iv), p. 12 [requirement; permission; criteria/list].** The plan shall include the type of additional instructional services and interventions the student **may receive**. Inclusion in the plan is mandatory; this clause describes possible receipt rather than an unconditional guarantee of every additional service.
- **K09 — (k)(2)(B)(v), p. 12 [requirement; criteria/list].** The plan shall include the **intensive, evidence-based literacy intervention program aligned to science of reading** the teacher will use to address **phonemic awareness, phonics, fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension**. All five areas are specified.
- **K10 — (k)(2)(B)(vi), p. 12 [requirement; permission; criteria/list].** The plan shall include strategies that the student's parents, legal guardians, or persons standing in loco parentis **are encouraged to use** to assist the student in achieving the reading goal. This is encouragement of family use, not a family mandate.
- **K11 — (k)(2)(B)(vii), p. 12 [requirement; authority; criteria/list].** The plan shall include **any additional services the teacher determines are available and appropriate** to accelerate reading-skill development. The teacher's availability/appropriateness determination is part of the component. K05–K11 are the seven specified main plan components; the skill examples under K05 are expressly nonexhaustive, and this final component covers additional qualifying services.
- **K12 — (k)(3), p. 12 [requirement; consequence; funding/timing].** Districts and charters shall notify all family recipients **in writing** of the content of their child's **“independent reading plan”** and progress on it **throughout the year**. “Independent” is the word printed here, while (k)(2) says “individual”; this inventory does not substitute one for the other.
- **K13 — (k)(4)(A)–(B), pp. 12–13 [requirement; consequence; criteria/list; funding/timing].** **By the beginning of the 2024–2025 school year**, districts and charters shall report to the division both (A) **types of interventions used** and (B) **students receiving each intervention type**. These are two required report components.

### (l): Promotion restriction, good cause, subsequent support (pages 13–15)

- **L01 — (l)(1), p. 13 [prohibition; consequence; authority; exception; funding/timing].** **By the beginning of the 2025–2026 school year**, the printed rule is: “if a public school student has not met the third-grade reading standard, as defined by the state board, **or** the student does not have a good-cause exemption, as provided under this subsection, the student shall not be promoted to grade four (4).” The emphasized connector is **or**, not an inferred “and”. Read alongside the express good-cause promotion permission in L02 without silently repairing the text.
- **L02 — (l)(2)(A), p. 13 [permission; authority; exception; consequence].** A grade-3 student not meeting the reading standard for promotion to grade 4 **may be promoted by the school district for good cause**. This is discretionary promotion, not automatic promotion on an exemption claim.
- **L03 — (l)(2)(B) introductory clause, pp. 13–14 [requirement; criteria/list; exception].** A good-cause exemption **shall be limited to** the six categories in L04–L09. This is a limited six-category list, **with a state-board-controlled residual category in (vi)**; it is not limited solely to the five specifically described student groups and is not an unrestricted local exemption power.
- **L04 — (l)(2)(B)(i), p. 13 [criteria/list; exception; funding/timing].** Category (i): **Limited English Proficiency students** with **less than three years of instruction in an English language learner program**.
- **L05 — (l)(2)(B)(ii)(a)–(b), p. 13 [criteria/list; exception; funding/timing; named source].** Category (ii): students **with a disability**, **not eligible for the alternate assessment**, with an **individualized education program or a 504 Plan** reflecting both (a) receipt of an intensive, evidence-based literacy intervention aligned to science of reading for **more than two years**, **and** (b) continued reading-proficiency need **or** previous retention in **K, grade 1, grade 2, or grade 3**. The “and” between (a) and (b), the “or” within (b), and “more than” two years all matter. The source names a 504 Plan but prints no expanded federal statutory citation for it.
- **L06 — (l)(2)(B)(iii)(a)–(d), pp. 13–14 [criteria/list; exception; funding/timing].** Category (iii) requires **all four**: (a) an intensive, evidence-based literacy intervention aligned to science of reading for **two or more years**; (b) continued reading-proficiency need **and** prior retention in **K, 1, 2, or 3**; (c) a special-education referral **and** full comprehensive evaluation; and (d) failure to meet exceptional-education criteria. This is not identical to category (ii), either in duration or conjunctions.
- **L07 — (l)(2)(B)(iv), p. 14 [criteria/list; exception; funding/timing].** Category (iv): students already retained in **K, grade 1, grade 2, or grade 3 for one year**.
- **L08 — (l)(2)(B)(v)(a), p. 14 [criteria/list; exception].** Category (v): students able to demonstrate they are **successful and independent readers** and can perform **at or above grade level**.
- **L09 — (l)(2)(B)(vi), p. 14 [authority; criteria/list; exception].** Category (vi): other students with **necessary, justifiable good-cause exemptions** identified as appropriate **by the state board in consultation with reading experts**. This is the residual category within the limited list.
- **L10 — (l)(2)(B)(v)(b), p. 14 [permission; requirement; authority; criteria/list; exception].** A district and charter **may use tools to reevaluate a student in accordance with state board rules**. The clause says those rules “shall include without limitation subsequent student assessments or alternative assessments”; retain this **open** assessment provision and do not make reevaluation itself mandatory.
- **L11 — (l)(3) introductory clause, p. 14 [requirement; consequence; criteria/list; funding/timing].** For **each student not meeting the state-board reading standard by the end of grade 3**, **including students promoted to grade 4 with good-cause exemptions**, the enrolling district or charter is subject to (A)–(F) **during the subsequent summer and school year**. The enumerated support provisions are L12–L18; exemption from retention is not exemption from this support scope.
- **L12 — (l)(3)(A), p. 14 [requirement; consequence; criteria/list; funding/timing].** The enrolling district or charter shall provide **at least 90 minutes of evidence-based literacy instruction aligned to science of reading during each school day** for the L11 population. This is a minimum daily duration, within the introductory summer-and-school-year scope.
- **L13 — (l)(3)(B)(i)–(ii), pp. 14–15 [requirement; criteria/list; exception; consequence; funding/timing; named source].** The entity shall assign the student to a teacher with a **value-added model score in the statewide top quartile in English language arts for the past three years**. **If unable to identify such a teacher**, it shall assign a teacher either (i) with a **highly-effective rating under the Teacher Excellence and Support System, § 6-17-2801 et seq., where possible**, or (ii) deemed high-performing as defined by a **Master Professional Educator designation**. The fallback is conditional on inability to identify the top-quartile teacher; “where possible” appears in (i). These are two enumerated fallback alternatives.
- **L14 — (l)(3)(C)(i), p. 15 [requirement; consequence; finding/purpose].** The entity shall give parents, legal guardians, or persons standing in loco parentis a **“read-at-home” plan to support early literacy growth**.
- **L15 — (l)(3)(C)(ii), p. 15 [requirement; criteria/list].** That plan shall include **evidence-based science-of-reading strategies and tools aligned to the student's individual reading plan**, for family recipients to use with their children. These are required plan contents; no exhaustive list of particular tools is given.
- **L16 — (l)(3)(D), p. 15 [requirement; consequence].** The entity shall identify students eligible for this section's literacy tutoring grants and notify parents, legal guardians, or persons standing in loco parentis of their child's eligibility.
- **L17 — (l)(3)(E), p. 15 [requirement; consequence; funding/timing].** The printed item is **“Be given priority to receive a literacy tutoring grant under this section”**. It appears under an introduction whose grammatical subject is the enrolling district or charter, although the surrounding provisions concern students. Preserve the grant-priority provision and that textual attribution issue rather than inventing a different operative sentence.
- **L18 — (l)(3)(F), p. 15 [requirement; permission; consequence; criteria/list].** The printed item is **“Be given the option to participate in additional intensive, evidence-based literacy intervention programs aligned to the science of reading.”** It has the same passive construction under the district/charter introduction as L17. The provision offers an option, not compulsory participation. L12–L18 collectively record the six specified (A)–(F) support components, with (C)'s content requirement recorded separately.

### (m): Division enforcement, rules, independent evaluation (page 15)

- **M01 — (m)(1), p. 15 [requirement; authority].** The division shall enforce this section.
- **M02 — (m)(2), p. 15 [requirement; authority].** The division shall promulgate rules to implement this section. This **shall** differs from the state board's **may** in (p).
- **M03 — (m)(3), p. 15 [requirement; authority; consequence; funding/timing; finding/purpose].** The division shall contract with a vendor for an **annual independent evaluation** to identify and assess strategies used by **the state, districts, and charters** to support Arkansas students in reading at grade level **by the end of grade 3**. This is the third of three enumerated division duties. No vendor, contract amount, evaluation submission recipient, or report deadline is named here.

### (n): Definitions (pages 15–16)

- **N01 — (n)(1), p. 15 [definition].** **“Science of reading”** means **“the study of the relationship between cognitive science and educational outcomes”**. Do not substitute an outside or narrower definition.
- **N02 — (n)(2), p. 16 [definition; criteria/list].** **“Structured literacy”** means **“an approach by which licensed personnel teach reading in an explicit, systematic, cumulative, and diagnostic manner.”** The licensed-personnel subject and all four characteristics belong to the definition. The two definitions are the closed enumeration in (n); the section's other express definition is “eligible student” in T02, with the descriptive MSV equivalence in I05.

### (o): Education Ombudsman (pages 16–17)

- **O01 — (o)(1)(A)–(C), p. 16 [requirement; authority; criteria/list; finding/purpose; named source].** The **Secretary of the Department of Education shall hire an Education Ombudsman** to assist the division in enforcing this section, including **without limitation** requirements for (A) demonstrating proficiency, (B) providing professional development, and (C) using a permitted instruction program. These three enforcement areas form an expressly **open** list.
- **O02 — (o)(2), p. 16 [permission; authority; criteria/list].** The secretary **may designate additional requirements related to public education**, including **without limitation enforcement of literacy requirements**. This is an open authorization; it is not restricted to the three (o)(1) examples.
- **O03 — (o)(3), p. 16 [requirement; prohibition; authority].** The secretary **shall supervise** the Education Ombudsman and **shall not delegate that supervision to a division employee**. Both the duty and the delegation prohibition are express.
- **O04 — (o)(4)(A)(i)–(ii), p. 16 [requirement; criteria/list].** The Ombudsman's **minimum qualifications shall include a master's degree in education or a related field**. The qualification is a minimum, not an exhaustive qualification list; the two specified degree-field alternatives include a related-field category.
- **O05 — (o)(4)(B), p. 16 [prohibition; criteria/list].** Anyone who has served as a past public school district superintendent **or** currently serves as one **is not eligible** to serve as Education Ombudsman. The exclusion covers both former and current superintendents.
- **O06 — (o)(5)(A)(i), p. 16 [permission; authority; criteria/list].** The Ombudsman **may communicate with a public school student**, **with permission from the student's parent, legal guardian, or person standing in loco parentis**. Permission is an express condition on this student-contact power.
- **O07 — (o)(5)(A)(ii), p. 16 [permission; authority; criteria/list].** The Ombudsman may communicate with a public school student's parent, legal guardian, or person standing in loco parentis.
- **O08 — (o)(5)(A)(iii), p. 17 [permission; authority; criteria/list].** The Ombudsman may communicate with **administration, faculty, and staff employed by a district or charter**. O06–O08 are the three enumerated communication-recipient categories, without an open-list formula.
- **O09 — (o)(5)(B), p. 17 [permission; authority].** The Ombudsman may review an issue or concern related to the education of a public school student **enrolled in a public school or open-enrollment public charter school**.
- **O10 — (o)(5)(C), p. 17 [permission; authority].** The Ombudsman may recommend training and resources to a **public school, district, or charter**. This is a recommendation power, not a mandate that recipients adopt the recommendation.
- **O11 — (o)(5)(D), p. 17 [permission; authority].** The Ombudsman may request support and assistance **from the division** to be provided to a **public school, district, or charter**. The clause authorizes a request; it does not expressly mandate granting it. (o)(5) enumerates four powers (A)–(D), without “including without limitation”.
- **O12 — (o)(6)(A), p. 17 [requirement; consequence; criteria/list; funding/timing].** The Ombudsman shall prepare and submit an **annual report to the state board** concerning **the Ombudsman's work and any recommendations related to the Ombudsman's focus areas**. Both subject areas are specified.
- **O13 — (o)(6)(B)(i)–(ii), p. 17 [requirement; consequence; criteria/list; funding/timing; named source].** That report **shall be submitted every two years** to **both the House Committee on Education and the Senate Committee on Education**. This is distinct from annual submission to the state board; the two committee recipients are a closed enumeration.

### (p): State board rules (page 17)

- **P01 — (p), p. 17 [permission; authority].** The state board **may** promulgate rules to implement this section. Do not change this permission to the division's mandatory rulemaking duty in M02.
