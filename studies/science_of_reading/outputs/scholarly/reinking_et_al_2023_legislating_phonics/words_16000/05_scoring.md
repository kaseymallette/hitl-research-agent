## Materials and scoring rules

Only `article.pdf`, `source.yaml`, `01_claims_reference.md`, `02_baseline_answers.md`, and `04_close_reading_answers.md` were opened as study inputs. I did not open the close-reading JSON mentioned inside Method B, consult cited works, or use outside sources. Article text was extracted with Apple PDFKit through Swift. I also inspected rendered images of PDF pages 4, 10–12, and 16 (printed pages 107, 113–115, and 119), including all three figures and the two ambiguous line-end hyphens.

The supplied relevance lists are fixed, including their overlapping and cumulative reference entries. No R+ claims were added. Recall is the number of distinct relevant R entries substantively used within the particular question’s answer, divided by that question’s fixed denominator. Quotations and explicit headings or figure paraphrases can supply usage; mere thematic similarity, supporting evidence not actually used by the answer, and content present only in another question cannot. The answers’ general bibliographic/provenance introductions are not treated as additional question-specific answers.

Usage does not require reproducing an entire reference quotation. A substantive assertion or qualification can be used in an excerpt. However, matching an inherited lead-in alone does not establish an added assertion in a cumulative entry: for example, mentioning the Orton-Gillingham meta-analysis uses R60 but does not establish R61’s additional What Works Clearinghouse assertion; quoting the no-settled-boundary sentence uses R259 but does not establish R260’s added statement about unanswered research questions. This same rule applies to both methods. A finding can be used without repeating every subsequent rhetorical question; that does not count the omitted questions as separately used claims.

For precision, the unit is each separately presented quotation occurrence (one outer quoted passage in an answer bullet), even when repeated or split across bullets. Nested quotation marks, cited titles, and scare quotes within that passage are not additional occurrences. All quotations are included. The three explicitly unquoted figure descriptions in A/Q3 count as paraphrases for recall, not as quotations for precision. The ledger below identifies every quotation by method, question, and bullet number; A3.30–32 identify the three unquoted descriptions.

Exactness concerns the article’s words, including its existing square brackets and ellipses. Whitespace, composed/decomposed Unicode, ligatures, line-wrap word divisions, outer quote delimiters, and superscript note markers are typographical differences, not wording failures. In particular, `ideology2`, `online7`, and the trailing `3` after the decodable-text quotation are article note callouts. The words `phonics-first` and `letter-sounds` retain their lexical hyphens where split over printed lines. All other wording and punctuation inside the excerpts were compared against the PDF text.

An excerpt can match a real reference claim for precision without establishing every added assertion of a longer cumulative entry for recall. Relevance passes if its substantive quotation content bears on the question and matches a claim in that question’s fixed list. Attribution is read from the answer’s explicit bullet labels and subsection framing. “Authors’ characterization of phonics-first advocates” identifies the advocates’ view; it does not endorse that view as the authors’ own position.
