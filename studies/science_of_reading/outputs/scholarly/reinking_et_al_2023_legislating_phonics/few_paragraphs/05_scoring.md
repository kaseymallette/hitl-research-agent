## Scope and counting rules

Only `article.pdf`, `source.yaml`, `01_claims_reference.md`, `02_baseline_answers.md`, and `04_close_reading_answers.md` were opened as inputs. No cited works, outside sources, or close-reading intermediate file were consulted. The PDF was read as text extracted with Apple PDFKit, invoked through Swift. Printed journal pagination is used below; journal p. 104 is PDF p. 1.

Recall is the number of distinct fixed relevant R entries substantively used within the corresponding question section, divided by that question’s fixed denominator. A quotation may use several R entries, and an entry counts once per question. A substantive excerpt from a compound entry counts as use of that entry, not as reproduction of every assertion in it. Thus, for example, quoting the disciplinary expertise sentence uses both R238 and R239, and quoting the professional-judgment sentence uses R247 and R248. This is entry-use recall, not a measure of exhaustive coverage within each compound entry. The question headings were also checked for substantive paraphrases; the shared bibliographic/introduction material was not assigned repeatedly to all three answers.

Near-verbatim restatements count: R3 and R48 express the same comprehensive, differentiated, multiple-approach proposition and are both credited where either is quoted. The definition in R26 also states the instructional primacy and sequencing in R30–R31; the advocates’ settled-science position uses R40 and the corresponding position reported in R98; and the synthetic-phonics prescription uses R35 as well as R175. The explicit commitment to teaching phonics uses the self-commitment assertion in R2. The comprehensive, balanced instruction quotation expressly includes flexibility for individual needs (R221); A1.16 quotes the NRP endorsement of balanced instruction (R222); and rejecting generalizations in unique cases expressly uses the general-versus-particular distinction in R246. General thematic resemblance, merely supporting another entry, and a reference entry’s Evidence field alone do not generate further recall credit. For example, the broad rhetoric quotation uses R273–R274 but does not automatically use every other entry criticizing politicization.

Precision counts each separately presented outer quotation once, including a three-sentence quotation as one quotation. Embedded quotation marks, module titles inside a longer quotation, and CR identifiers do not create additional quotations. Repeated quotations used under different questions are separate uses. Every quotation is audited below for all four required properties. A quotation can match a real claim outside the fixed relevant list; it would then fail relevance. None does so here.

Exactness was checked against the extracted PDF text on the cited page(s), allowing line wraps, rejoined line-end word divisions, running-header removal, equivalent curly/straight quotation marks, and the presence or absence of superscript note callouts. No lexical substitutions or unmarked substantive omissions were accepted. A1.7 retains note 10 and A1.11 retains note 7; their absence in Method B is not an inexact quotation. B3.4 crosses pp. 120–121, where PDFKit inserts a running header; B3.6 contains “letter-sounds,” split as “letter-” / “sounds” at a printed line boundary. Both passages were checked in context and are exact.

For attribution, “authors’ characterization of advocates’ position, which they dispute” is a correct description of the authors reporting the other side; it does not claim the authors endorse that position. Conversely, an entry marked disputing whose words are the authors’ own criticism is correctly attributed as the authors’ view or critique. NRP passages are identified as NRP words endorsed by the authors.
