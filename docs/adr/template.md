## Context

What was true that forced a choice. The constraint, the failure, or the
question that had no default answer. Written as it stood at the time, in the
past tense, without the benefit of what happened next.

## Decision

The decision itself, in the present tense, as a rule the code is expected to
follow. One decision per record. If two things were decided together and
either could be reversed without the other, they are two records.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. An alternative dismissed in one
line is still worth recording — the point is that a later reader can see it
was considered rather than missed.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it costs. Include the costs
honestly: a decision with no downside recorded is usually a decision that was
not examined.

## Notes

Where a decision is later reversed, add a line here pointing at the record that
supersedes it — and change **Status** to `Superseded by ADR-NNNN`. Nothing else
in this file is edited after it is accepted.
